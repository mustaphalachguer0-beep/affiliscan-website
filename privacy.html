Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how AffiliScan ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the AffiliScan Chrome extension and website at affiliscan.app.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

AffiliScan is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal information
  • Your browsing history or YouTube watch history
  • Your Amazon account details or affiliate credentials
  • Any financial information
  • Analytics or usage data

2. How the Extension Works

AffiliScan operates entirely within your browser. Here is exactly what it does:

  • Reads YouTube descriptions — only when you click "Scan Description" on a YouTube video tab. It reads only the video description to find Amazon affiliate links.
  • Fetches Amazon pages — it makes direct requests to Amazon product pages to check if links are working. These requests go directly from your browser to Amazon, not through any server we control.
  • Uses YouTube Data API — for bulk scanning, it calls the YouTube Data API to fetch video descriptions. This uses a public API key and does not require or access your Google account.
  • Local storage only — all scan results are saved in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local). This data never leaves your device.

3. Permissions Explained

AffiliScan requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • activeTab — to read the current YouTube video tab when you click Scan
  • scripting — to inject the content script that reads the video description
  • storage — to save scan results locally in your browser
  • tabs — to find the active YouTube tab

4. Third-Party Services

The extension interacts with the following third-party services:

  • Amazon.com and regional Amazon sites — to check if affiliate links are working
  • YouTube Data API v3 (Google) — for bulk scanning playlists and channels

These services have their own privacy policies. We do not share any data with them beyond what is necessary to check link status.

5. Children's Privacy

AffiliScan is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

7. Contact
