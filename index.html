The problem

Every broken link is
lost commission

Amazon affiliate links break silently. Products get discontinued, tags get stripped, listings disappear. You have no idea until someone tells you.

💀
Broken links
Product pages that return 404, redirect to homepage, or have had their affiliate tag stripped. Every click earns you nothing.
🚫
Unavailable products
Items still listed but marked "Currently unavailable" — visitors can't buy, so you can't earn. These are invisible without scanning.
📺
Hundreds of old videos
Your video from 3 years ago still gets traffic. But the affiliate links? Half are dead. Checking them manually takes days.
